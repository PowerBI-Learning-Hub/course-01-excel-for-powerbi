# Starting a Career in Data Analytics with Power BI

## Core Idea

This lecture explains:

* Why data analytics is a high-demand career
* How businesses rely on data
* Why Power BI skills are valuable
* How beginners can start a career in data analytics
* Real examples of people transitioning into the field

Simple understanding:

> Companies collect massive amounts of data, and data analysts help turn that data into business decisions.

---

## Why This Matters

Modern businesses rely heavily on data.

Without analysis, data has little value.

Organizations use data to:

* Improve customer experience
* Increase profits
* Optimize operations
* Predict trends
* Make smarter decisions

Because of this:

> Skilled data analysts are in high demand across almost every industry.

## Power Tips

### Start Small

Don’t try learning everything at once.

Best progression:

1. Excel basics
2. Data cleaning
3. Power BI basics
4. DAX
5. Dashboards

---

### Build Real Projects

Portfolio projects matter a lot for entry-level jobs.

Examples:

* Sales dashboard
* HR analytics report
* Retail analysis dashboard

---

### Learn Business Thinking

Technical skills alone are not enough.

Always ask:

> “What business problem am I solving?”

---

### Consistency Beats Intensity

Daily practice is more effective than occasional long study sessions.
