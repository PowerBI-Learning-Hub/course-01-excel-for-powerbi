# Introduction to Data Analytics & Microsoft Power BI Career Path

## Core Idea

This lecture explains:

* What data analytics is
* Why data matters in everyday life and business
* What a data analyst actually does
* How Microsoft Excel and Power BI are used
* What skills you’ll learn in the Microsoft Power BI Analyst Professional Certificate
* How the course prepares you for the **PL-300 certification**

Simple understanding:

> Data analysts collect data, clean it, organize it, analyze it, and turn it into useful business insights.

Every company today uses data:

* Sales tracking

* Understand data

## Key Concepts

### What is Data Analytics?

Data analytics means:

1. Collecting data
2. Cleaning and organizing data
3. Analyzing patterns
4. Creating insights
5. Helping businesses make decisions

---

### What Does a Data Analyst Do?

A data analyst typically:

* Gathers data from different sources
* Cleans messy data
* Transforms data into usable format
* Builds reports and dashboards
* Finds trends and patterns
* Helps stakeholders make decisions

---

## Practical Understanding

### Everyday Example → Data Analytics

#### Vacation Planning

You compare:

* Flight prices
* Hotel costs
* Travel dates

That is basic data analysis.

---

#### Party Scheduling

You gather:

* Everyone’s availability
* Preferred dates

Then choose the best option.

Again → simple data analysis.

---

### Business Version of the Same Process

A company might analyze:

* Which product sells best
* Which region makes highest revenue
* Why sales dropped last month
* Which customers are likely to leave

The process is the same:

> Gather → Analyze → Decide

## Common Mistakes

### Beginners Often:

* Focus only on visuals
* Ignore data cleaning
* Build poor data models
* Use too many visuals in reports
* Skip storytelling
* Memorize DAX without understanding business logic
